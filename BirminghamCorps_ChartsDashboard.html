What you will have after this setup: Five shareable Google Form links your team fills out daily from any device. Every submission saves automatically to one Google Sheet. That sheet powers live charts that update on their own — no manual data entry in the sheet needed.
5-Step Setup — Takes About 30 Minutes Total
1
Go to forms.google.com and sign into your Google account. Click the large + button to create a new blank form. Repeat this five times — one form per section below.
2
Copy the questions from each section below into Google Forms. For each question, paste the title, set the type (Short answer, Paragraph, Dropdown, Date), and mark it Required if shown.
3
Connect each form to Google Sheets. Inside the form, click the green Sheets icon at the top → select "Create a new spreadsheet." Name it "Birmingham Corps — [Form Name]." Every submission now saves there automatically in real time.
4
Get each form's shareable link. Click Send → the chain-link icon → copy the link. Send that link to your team in a text, email, or pin it in Monday.com. Anyone with the link can fill it out — no Google account needed.
5
Share your Google Sheet with the team. Open the connected spreadsheet → click Share → change to "Anyone with the link can edit." Now both managers can view live data, add notes, and see every entry as it comes in.
Pro tip for auto-updating charts: In your Google Sheet, add a tab called "Summary." Use COUNTIF and SUM formulas there to count placements, hours, etc. Then insert Google Sheets charts from that Summary tab. Every time a form is submitted, the sheet updates and your charts refresh automatically with zero extra work.
